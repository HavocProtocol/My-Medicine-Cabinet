# 💊 Home Medicine Cabinet
### Your household pharmacy, organized. Your medication schedule, simplified.

Keep your medicines organized, track expiration dates, manage household inventory, and stay on top of your medication schedule — all from one convenient Android application.

**Home Medicine Cabinet** is designed to make managing a household medicine cabinet easier, smarter, and more reliable. From scanning medicine packaging to receiving medication reminders, it brings essential organization and safety features together in one place.

🌍 **Available in 4 languages:** English, العربية, Français, Deutsch  
📱 **Platform:** Android  
🎨 **Interface:** Material Design 3 · Light & Dark Mode  
💾 **Storage:** Offline-first local database

---

## ✨ Why Home Medicine Cabinet?

Ever found an expired medicine hiding at the back of a drawer? Forgotten how many tablets were left in a box? Or struggled to remember when a medication was supposed to be taken?

Home Medicine Cabinet helps you tackle these everyday problems with a collection of practical tools designed around your household's medicine inventory and medication routines.

- 📦 **Organize your entire medicine cabinet** in one place.
- 📸 **Scan medicine boxes and barcodes** to make registration easier.
- ⏰ **Set up medication schedules** with customizable reminders.
- ⚠️ **Stay informed about expiration dates** before medicines become a problem.
- 👨👩👧👦 **Manage medicines for your household** using family member profiles.
- 🌐 **Explore additional medicine information** through supported online sources.
- 🔒 **Keep your cabinet accessible offline** with locally stored data.

## 🚀 Features

### 📦 1. Smart Medicine Inventory

Say goodbye to guessing what you have in your medicine drawer.

Keep your household pharmacy organized with detailed records for each medicine and its inventory.

**What you can track:**
- Medicine and scientific names.
- Active ingredient information, when available.
- Strength and dosage form, such as tablets, capsules, syrups, drops, and creams.
- Remaining quantity and unit.
- Expiration date and batch number.
- Manufacturer and storage location.
- Refrigeration requirements.
- Personal notes and therapeutic categories.

**Quick inventory overview**

Your dashboard highlights:
- 🟢 Available medicines.
- 🟠 Medicines approaching expiration.
- 🔴 Expired medicines.

Search by medicine name, scientific name, or barcode, and filter your inventory by category or status to find what you need faster.

### 📸 2. Scan Medicine Packaging

Registering medicines doesn't have to mean typing every detail manually.

Home Medicine Cabinet includes scanning tools designed to help extract information from medicine packaging.

**Barcode Scanner**
- Scan supported medicine barcodes using your camera.
- Search for matching records in available local or online sources.

**Multi-Side Medicine Box Scanner**
- Capture the front, back, and side or flap of a medicine box.
- Extract potential medicine names, strengths, manufacturers, batch numbers, and storage instructions.
- Recognize expiration dates in supported formats.
- Handle Arabic and English text and numeral variations.
- Display confidence indicators to help identify uncertain results.

**You're always in control.**

Scanned information must be reviewed and confirmed before it is saved. The scanner is an assistant for data entry, not a substitute for checking the actual packaging.

### ⏰ 3. Medication Schedules & Smart Reminders

Build a medication routine around the instructions you've received from your healthcare professional.

Create schedules using medicines already registered in your cabinet, then configure the details of each routine.

**Flexible scheduling**
- Set start and optional end dates.
- Choose daily, selected-weekday, or interval-based schedules.
- Configure multiple reminder times per day.
- Set different prescribed dosages for individual time slots.
- Add administration instructions and personal notes.
- Enable, pause, resume, or discontinue schedules.

**Native Android notifications**

Receive reminders containing the scheduled medicine, configured dosage, time, and relevant instructions.

When a reminder arrives, you can:
- ✅ Mark the dose as taken.
- ⏭️ Mark it as skipped.
- 💤 Snooze the reminder.

**Your daily medication timeline**

See what's coming up, review completed doses, and identify missed or skipped doses.

The calendar lets you explore previous and upcoming schedules, while your medication history provides a record of dose activity.

**Inventory integration**

Optionally deduct the recorded dose from your inventory when you mark it as taken. Low-stock warnings help you notice when supplies may be running out.

> **Important:** The app records the dosage you enter; it does not determine or recommend a medically appropriate dose. Always follow your prescription or instructions from a qualified healthcare professional.

### 🛡️ 4. Expiration Tracking & Safety Alerts

Expired medicines shouldn't go unnoticed.

Home Medicine Cabinet monitors recorded expiration dates and helps you identify medicines that need attention.

**Expiration alerts**
- 🟠 30 days before expiration.
- 🟠 7 days before expiration.
- 🔴 On the recorded expiration date.

**Additional safeguards**
- Highlight expired medicines in your inventory.
- Warn when a medicine is approaching expiration.
- Flag missing or invalid expiration information.
- Block medication schedules for medicines that have already expired.
- Suspend future reminders when a scheduled medicine reaches its recorded expiration date.
- Block marking doses as taken when their medicine has expired.
- Prevent schedules from extending beyond a medicine's expiration date.

If a medicine expires while a schedule is active, the app is designed to suspend future reminders and flag pending doses as blocked.

These safeguards help you notice potential problems before relying on an outdated medication record.

**A safety reminder:** Expiration checks depend on accurate information. Always verify the date and condition of the actual medicine packaging. An unexpired medicine is not automatically appropriate or safe for every person.

### 🌐 5. Online Medicine Information

Want to learn more about a medicine in your cabinet?

The app can retrieve additional information from supported public sources, including:

- **OpenFDA** — drug-label information, including available indications and warnings.
- **NIH RxNorm** — normalized medicine and generic-name information.
- **Wikipedia** — accessible medicine summaries in supported languages.

Review source attribution and access the original information where available. You can also save selected information to your local medicine record.

Online lookup requires an internet connection, and the information available varies by source and medicine.

**This feature provides informational references, not a medical diagnosis or personalized treatment recommendation.** Always consult a qualified healthcare professional when making decisions about medication use.

### 👨👩👧👦 6. Family Medicine Management

Keep household medication records organized with family member profiles.

- Create profiles for household members.
- Associate inventory items with the appropriate person.
- Organize medicine records by family member.
- Record relevant household notes.
- Identify child profiles for additional pediatric-safety considerations.

Family profiles help you organize your household's medicine cabinet in one place.

**Important:** A child's profile flag is not a substitute for pediatric dosage verification. Never assume a medicine or dosage is appropriate for a child based solely on the information stored in the app.

### 🗂️ 7. Categories, Search & Organization

Keep everything easy to find, even as your inventory grows.

- Use default therapeutic categories.
- Create custom categories.
- Assign colors and icons for quick visual identification.
- Search by medicine name, scientific name, or barcode.
- Filter the inventory using category and availability indicators.
- View detailed medicine records with inventory and expiration information.

Less time searching through drawers. More time knowing what you have.

### 🌙 8. A Comfortable, Multilingual Experience

Your medicine cabinet should be easy to use, whatever your preferred language or display settings.

**Four supported languages**
- 🇬🇧 English
- 🇪🇬 العربية
- 🇫🇷 Français
- 🇩🇪 Deutsch

Switch languages from within the app. Arabic uses a right-to-left interface, while English, French, and German use left-to-right layouts.

**Choose your appearance**
- ☀️ Light Mode
- 🌙 Dark Mode

The interface uses Material Design 3 with clear status indicators, readable layouts, and a clean medical-inspired visual style.

### 💾 9. Offline-First Storage & Backup

Your medicine inventory is stored locally using Room, an SQLite-based database solution for Android.

This means core inventory functions are designed to remain accessible without an internet connection.

- View and manage locally stored medicine records.
- Review inventory quantities and recorded expiration dates.
- Access locally saved medication schedules and dose history.
- Export a JSON backup.
- Restore information from a compatible backup.

Some features, particularly online medicine lookup, require an internet connection. Notification delivery also depends on Android permissions, device settings, and platform behavior.

**Backup tip:** Export backups regularly and store them somewhere safe. Restoring a backup can replace or modify existing information depending on the implemented restore behavior, so review the app's confirmation messages carefully.

## 🔐 Privacy & Data Handling

Home Medicine Cabinet is designed around offline-first local storage.

- Core medicine records are stored in a local database.
- Online information lookup uses supported external services.
- Scanned packaging information is reviewed before being saved.
- Medicine records can include personal notes and family associations.
- Medication schedules and dose history may contain sensitive personal information.

Keep your device secure and protect any exported backups.

**Note:** Local storage does not, by itself, guarantee complete privacy. Online requests may transmit search terms or other request data to external services. Review the app's actual network behavior and privacy documentation before entering sensitive information.

# 🛠️ Build from Source

Want to build Home Medicine Cabinet yourself, contribute to development, or install a locally built version? Follow the instructions below.

## 📋 Prerequisites

Before getting started, make sure you have:

- **Git** — to clone the repository.
- **Android Studio** — the recommended IDE for Android development.
- **JDK** — the Java Development Kit version required by the project's Gradle configuration.
- **Android SDK** — the SDK platform and build tools required by the project.

You can download the necessary tools here:

- [Git](https://git-scm.com/downloads)
- [Android Studio](https://developer.android.com/studio)
- [JDK](https://adoptium.net/)

> **Note:** The required JDK version, Android SDK version, and Gradle configuration depend on the project's build files. Use the versions specified by the project rather than assuming the latest versions are compatible.

## 1. Clone the Repository

The official repository is:

**Repository URL:** https://github.com/HavocProtocol/My-Medicine-Cabinet

**Repository directory:** `My-Medicine-Cabinet`

Open a terminal, Command Prompt, or PowerShell and run:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/HavocProtocol/My-Medicine-Cabinet.git
```

Navigate into the cloned project directory:

```bash
cd My-Medicine-Cabinet
```

Git creates the local project directory when it clones the repository.

## 2. Open the Project in Android Studio

1. Launch Android Studio.
2. Select **Open**.
3. Navigate to the `My-Medicine-Cabinet` directory created when you cloned the repository.
4. Select the project folder containing the Gradle build files.
5. Allow Android Studio to synchronize the project and download any required dependencies.

If Android Studio requests a specific JDK or Android SDK installation, install or select the version required by the project.

Wait for Gradle synchronization to finish before proceeding.

## 3. Build the Application

Once Gradle synchronization succeeds, you can build the application directly from Android Studio.

1. Open the **Build** menu.
2. Select **Make Project** or the equivalent build action in your Android Studio version.
3. Wait for the build process to finish.
4. Check the Build output for errors.

Alternatively, you can build from the terminal using the Gradle Wrapper included in the repository.

First, make sure your terminal is inside the `My-Medicine-Cabinet` directory.

**Windows — PowerShell or Command Prompt:**

```powershell
.\gradlew.bat assembleDebug
```

**Linux or macOS:**

```bash
chmod +x gradlew
./gradlew assembleDebug
```

These commands compile the debug version of the application and package it into an APK, provided the project builds successfully.

## 4. Find the Generated APK

After a successful debug build, the APK is typically generated at:

```text
app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
```

The exact output path may vary if the project uses a different module name or custom build configuration.

You can transfer the APK to a compatible Android device and install it, subject to the device's security settings.

**Tip:** Debug APKs are intended for development and testing. For a release build suitable for distribution, configure the appropriate release build type and signing credentials.

## 5. Install on a Connected Android Device

To install the debug APK using Android Debug Bridge (ADB):

1. Enable **Developer options** on your Android device.
2. Enable **USB debugging**.
3. Connect the device to your computer.
4. Make sure ADB recognizes the device.
5. Run the following command from the project directory:

```bash
adb install -r app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
```

The `-r` option reinstalls the application while attempting to preserve its existing application data.

Alternatively, transfer the APK to your device and open it to install manually, allowing installation from that source if Android requests permission.

## 6. Update Your Local Repository

If you already cloned the repository and want to retrieve the latest changes, navigate to the project directory and run:

```bash
cd My-Medicine-Cabinet
git pull
```

Then rebuild the application using Android Studio or the Gradle Wrapper.

If you have local modifications, review your changes before pulling updates to avoid conflicts.

## 🧰 Troubleshooting

### Gradle synchronization fails

- Check your internet connection.
- Confirm that the configured JDK is compatible with the project's Gradle version.
- Install the Android SDK platforms and build tools required by the project.
- Review the Gradle error output for missing dependencies or configuration problems.

### The build fails

- Review the first meaningful error in the Build output.
- Verify that the repository contains the Gradle Wrapper and all required project files.
- Check whether the project requires additional configuration or local properties.
- Retry the build after resolving the reported issue.

### The APK cannot be installed

- Confirm that the APK was generated successfully.
- Verify that the Android device supports the app's minimum SDK version.
- Check for signing conflicts with an already installed version.
- Confirm that Android permits installation from the selected source.

### The application builds but some features do not work

- Check Android notification permissions and alarm-related settings.
- Verify camera permissions for barcode and medicine-box scanning.
- Remember that online medicine lookup requires internet access.
- Check the application's runtime logs for errors.

## 🛡️ A Note on Medication Safety

Home Medicine Cabinet is an organizational and informational tool. It is **not a substitute for a doctor, pharmacist, prescription label, or emergency medical service**.

The app does not independently establish that a medicine is appropriate for a person, verify every dosage, or guarantee that two medicines can be taken together.

Always:
- Follow your prescription and the instructions on the medicine packaging.
- Verify medicine names, strengths, and expiration dates.
- Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure about a medication.
- Seek appropriate medical assistance for urgent symptoms or suspected medication errors.

Automated warnings are intended to support careful medicine management, not replace professional judgment.
